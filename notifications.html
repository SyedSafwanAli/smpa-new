Instruments Constituting the Authority

These notifications and orders constitute the Sindh Master Plan Authority and define its mandate. Most recent first.

  • Transfer of the Department of Planning & Urban Design (DPUD) to the Karachi Development Authority

    SO(G)/HTP/KDA/8-23/2022 18-10-2022 LG&HTP Department, Government of Sindh

    2022

  • Merger of the Department of Town Planning, Government of Sindh, into the Sindh Master Plan Authority

    SO(G)/HTP/SBCA/7-63/2018 14-09-2020 LG&HTP Department, Government of Sindh

    2020

  • Establishment of a separate “Sindh Master Plan Authority”

    SO(G)/HTP/SBCA/7-63/2018 18-02-2020 LG&HTP Department, Government of Sindh

    2020

  • Order of the Honourable Supreme Court of Pakistan

    C.P 815-K/2016 22-01-2019 Supreme Court of Pakistan

    2019

  • Placement of the Master Plan Department under the Administrative, Financial and Technical control of the Director General, SBCA

    SO(L&C)HTP/SBCA/12-14/2013 06-11-2013 HTP Department, Government of Sindh

    2013
