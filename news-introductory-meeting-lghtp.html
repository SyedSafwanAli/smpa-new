Introductory Meeting Chaired by the Caretaker Minister LG&HTP

  • 15 April 2026
  • Sindh Master Plan Authority

An introductory meeting was chaired by the Caretaker Minister for Local Government, Housing and Town Planning (LG&HTP), the department under which the Sindh Master Plan Authority operates.

The record

Event
Introductory meeting
Chaired by
Caretaker Minister for Local Government, Housing & Town Planning, Government of Sindh
Held at
Local Government and Housing Town Planning Department
Published
15 April 2026, by the Sindh Master Plan Authority

Context

LG&HTP is the department the Authority answers to: the merger of the Town Planning Department into the Authority was notified by it under SO(G)/HTP/SBCA/7-63/2018, and its Minister chairs the Authority’s own reviews — the draft Sindh Master Plan Authority Act among them. An introductory meeting is the first sitting at which a newly appointed minister is briefed by the department and its attached authorities.

The Authority’s post for this event carries the headline, the date and the still above — no account of the proceedings was published. This page reproduces that record rather than reconstructing the meeting.
