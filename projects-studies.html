  • Planning work across the cities and districts of Sindh

What the Authority Undertakes

Following the merger of the Department of Town Planning, the Authority carries the master planning mandate for all districts of Sindh, Karachi division included. The kinds of project and study undertaken under that mandate are set out below.

01

Master & Development Plans

Preparation of master plans, development plans, strategic plans and structure plans for any area, city, district or division.

02

Zonal & Land Use Plans

Determining zones, land-uses and control measures, and designating residential, commercial, industrial and recreational areas.

03

Surveys & Analyses

Review of existing conditions, surveys and analyses, on which the goals, strategies and financial implications of each plan are founded.

04

Contingency Plans

Plans providing a foundation for administration, development control, and the preparation or amendment of regulations.
