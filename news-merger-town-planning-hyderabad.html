Merger of the Town Planning Department, Hyderabad, into SMPA

  • 15 April 2026
  • Sindh Master Plan Authority

The Department of Town Planning, Government of Sindh, having the mandate of master planning of the overall districts of Sindh (except Karachi division), merged into the Sindh Master Plan Authority vide LG&HTP Department’s Notification SO(G)/HTP/SBCA/7-63/2018 dated 14-09-2020.

The record

Merged entity
Department of Town Planning, Hyderabad, Government of Sindh
Merged into
Sindh Master Plan Authority (SMPA)
Notification
No. SO(G)/HTP/SBCA/7-63/2018 dated 14-09-2020
Issued by
Local Government, Housing & Town Planning (LG&HTP) Department, Government of Sindh
Mandate transferred
Master planning of the overall districts of Sindh, except Karachi division

The merger consolidated master planning for the province under one authority: Karachi division through the Authority’s Karachi offices, and every other district through the town planning establishment at Hyderabad. The Authority’s directorates for town planning outside Karachi date from this notification.
