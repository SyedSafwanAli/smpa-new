Establishment of SMPA in Pursuance of the Supreme Court of Pakistan’s Order

  • 15 April 2026
  • Sindh Master Plan Authority

In pursuance of the Supreme Court of Pakistan’s Order dated 22-01-2019 and the subsequent Cabinet decision, the Master Plan Department was separated from SBCA and established as a separate Sindh Master Plan Authority vide letter dated 18-02-2020.

The record

Supreme Court order
Dated 22-01-2019, passed in C.P # 815-K of 2016
Cabinet decision
Taken by the Sindh Cabinet in its meeting held on 22-01-2020
Administrative notification
No. SO(G)/HTP/SBCA/7-63/2018 dated 18-02-2020
Separated from
Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA)
Established as
Sindh Master Plan Authority (SMPA), formerly the Master Plan Group of Offices (MPGO)
Powers exercised under
Clauses 1-4 and 3A-1 of the Karachi Building & Town Planning Regulations, 2002 (as amended)

The separation gave the Authority its own mandate over master planning and town planning functions that had previously sat inside the building control organisation. The Gazette notification reproduced above is the published instrument in which the Authority itself recites that chain of authority — the Court’s order, the Cabinet’s decision and the administrative notification that followed.
