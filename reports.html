The Authority’s Research Function

Performing analysis, conducting research and publishing reports on all aspects, factors and components of sustainable and liveable urban areas is one of the Authority’s stated functions.

In ideal scenarios urban land is never used, allotted, auctioned or leased without carrying out studies, surveys and other logical exercise. Behind every master plan and land use plan therefore stands the survey, analysis and strategy work that this page will carry.

  • Research reports on the components of sustainable urban areas
  • Surveys and analyses supporting the master plans and land use plans
  • Policy and strategy documents
  • Annual performance reports of the Authority
