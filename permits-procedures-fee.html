Permits, Procedures & Fee

  • Development permits and town planning NOCs

Two Categories of Town Planning Work

SBCO-1979, KB&TPR-1979, KB&TPR-2002 and RIB&TPR-2018 generally divide town planning functions in Sindh into two main categories.

Master Planning Work

Preparation of Master Plan, Development Plan, Strategic Plan, Structural Plan etc., including contingency plans, for the purpose of providing a foundation for the administration, development control and preparation or amendment of regulations.

Land Development Work

Land development carried out through General Development Permits, Special Development Permits and Town Planning NOCs.

Permits and NOCs

  • Schemes

    Site development scheme, housing scheme, development scheme, dividing land into suitable plots, housing / industrial / commercial / public purpose schemes, and assigning land-use for urban areas.

  • Special Development Permits

    Major sub-division, approval of any new planning or layout plan, change of land-use, and similar matters.

  • General Development Permits

    Minor sub-division and amalgamations.

  • Town Planning NOCs

    Petrol pumps, CNG, LNG, marriage halls, super stores, farm houses and similar uses.

Which work falls to whom

The work regarding Macro Planning / Master Planning, issuance of special development permits, change of land-use and other similar work are sole purview of MP&EC / MPGO / MPD (now SMPA). In other districts of Sindh the Master Planning tasks pertain to the Directorate of Town Planning (now merged in SMPA). The concerned Authorities (notified by Government) may issue General Development Permits and draw schemes in their jurisdictions but only in accordance with the Master Plan of the City. The Concerned Authority shall simultaneously endorse a copy of each General Development Permit granted by it to MP&ECD (now SMPA).

Fee Schedule

A prescribed fee applies to each permit and NOC. The current rates will be published in the table below.

General Development Permits Minor sub-division and amalgamation Rate not yet published
