About Us

  • Sindh Master Plan Authority

Vision and Mission

Empowering Sindh's Urban Landscape for Sustainable Development and Dynamic Economic Growth

At SMPA, we aim to provide long and short-term plans, strategies and framework to transform the cities of Sindh into self-sufficient, sustainable and resilient cities, holding viable and improving economy, providing aesthetic recreational and public spaces, promoting diversity and ecological balance, preserving culture and environment.

Our Vision

Empowering Sustainable Progress: Transforming Sindh into a model of inclusive, resilient, and harmoniously developed communities through comprehensive planning and strategic implementation.

Our Mission

At Sindh Master Plan Authority, our mission is to lead the way in fostering holistic and equitable development across Sindh, driven by innovative urban planning, robust infrastructure, and environmental stewardship. We strive to collaborate with diverse stakeholders, leverage cutting-edge technologies, and prioritize the needs of our communities to create a vibrant, progressive, and sustainable province for generations to come.

Vision of the Sindh Master Plan Authority

Fostering Sustainable Urban Excellence for Prosperity and Well-being

The Sindh Master Plan Authority envisions a future where the cities and urban areas within the province of Sindh are exemplars of sustainable urban excellence, contributing significantly to economic growth, social well-being, and environmental stewardship. Our vision integrates economic prosperity as a pivotal element in the urban development equation.

01

Inclusive and Prosperous Economy

SMPA aspires to create cities that serve as economic engines, providing a conducive environment for businesses to flourish. Our vision includes vibrant urban economies that generate jobs, attract investments, and stimulate entrepreneurship, thereby improving the livelihoods of all residents.

02

Inclusive and Equitable Communities

We envision cities where economic opportunities are accessible to all, regardless of socio-economic background. Inclusivity and social equity are integral to our vision, ensuring that the benefits of urban development are widely shared.

03

Sustainability and Environmental Stewardship

SMPA is dedicated to building cities that prioritize sustainability, balancing economic growth with environmental conservation. Our vision includes eco-friendly infrastructure, green spaces, and resource-efficient practices that promote a healthy environment for current and future generations.

04

Technological Advancement

We embrace technological innovation to enhance economic productivity and urban living. Our vision includes smart cities that leverage technology to improve infrastructure, transportation, communication, and governance, fostering economic competitiveness.

05

Collaborative Partnerships

SMPA envisions strong partnerships with communities, government agencies, private-sector stakeholders, and civil society organizations. Collaborative efforts drive economic development and ensure that urban planning aligns with the diverse needs of stakeholders.

06

Resilience and Adaptability

Our vision includes cities that are resilient to economic shocks, natural disasters, and global challenges. SMPA aims to create urban environments that can adapt and thrive in a dynamic and ever-changing economic landscape.

07

Cultural Preservation and Celebration

We value and celebrate the cultural diversity of Sindh. Our vision integrates cultural elements into urban planning, enhancing the uniqueness of our cities and contributing to cultural tourism and creative industries that boost the economy.

08

Transparency and Accountability

SMPA is committed to transparent governance, ensuring that our economic decisions and actions are open to scrutiny and input from the public. Transparency and accountability are foundational principles for building trust and effective economic development.

09

Quality of Life

Above all, our vision focuses on improving the overall quality of life for residents. We aim to create cities where people live, work, and prosper in environments that promote well-being, happiness, and economic prosperity.

SMPA’s vision is a dynamic and forward-looking guide that drives our commitment to sustainable urban development and economic growth. We are dedicated to realizing this vision in partnership with all stakeholders, as we work together to transform Sindh’s cities into vibrant centers of economic excellence and well-being for generations to come.

Statement on Behalf of the Authority

Shaping Sustainable Urban Futures

As the Sindh Master Plan Authority, our mission is to shape the future of urban development in the province of Sindh. We are committed to creating sustainable, inclusive, and vibrant cities that enhance the quality of life for all residents. We recognize that urban planning is not merely about physical infrastructure; it is about fostering communities, preserving culture, and safeguarding the environment. Our approach is rooted in the following principles:

01

Inclusivity

We are dedicated to ensuring that our urban planning efforts benefit all residents, regardless of their background, socioeconomic status, or location within the province. Inclusivity is at the heart of every plan and policy we formulate.

02

Sustainability

We understand the critical importance of sustainable development. Our plans prioritize efficient land use, green spaces, and eco-friendly infrastructure to reduce our ecological footprint and protect our natural resources for generations to come.

03

Viable Economy

SMPA envisions cities and urban areas within the province of Sindh as dynamic hubs of economic activity, innovation, and prosperity. Our vision is to drive a resilient and constantly improving urban economy that positions Sindh as a leader in economic development.

04

Innovation

In a rapidly evolving world, embracing technological advancements and innovative solutions is imperative. SMPA continually explores cutting-edge technologies and urban planning methodologies to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of our initiatives.

05

Collaboration

SMPA believes in the power of collaboration. We actively engage with communities, government agencies, private-sector partners, and civil society organizations to foster a collective effort towards building better cities.

06

Resilience

As we confront the challenges posed by climate change and urbanization, we prioritize building resilient cities that can withstand shocks and adapt to changing circumstances while safeguarding the well-being of our citizens.

07

Transparency

We are committed to transparency in our decision-making processes, ensuring that the public is well-informed about our plans, projects, and policies. We encourage feedback and participation from all stakeholders.

08

Cultural Preservation

Sindh has a rich cultural heritage, and we are dedicated to preserving and celebrating its diversity. Our plans respect and integrate cultural elements that define our cities and communities.

SMPA’s work is driven by a vision of cities that are not just spaces for habitation but vibrant hubs of opportunity, creativity, and sustainability. Our commitment is unwavering, and we look forward to partnering with all stakeholders to achieve this vision. Together, we can build a future where the cities of Sindh are shining examples of urban planning excellence, offering the best possible quality of life for all their residents.
