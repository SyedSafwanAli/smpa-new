Chain of Command

The Sindh Master Plan Authority works under the Local Government and Housing & Town Planning Department, Government of Sindh. Its line of authority runs as follows.

Syed Murad Ali Shah

Chief Minister, Sindh

Nasir Hussain Shah

Minister, Local Government, Sindh

Dr. Waseem Shamshad Ali

Additional Chief Secretary, LG&HTP, GoS

Shakeel Siddiqui

Senior Director, Sindh Master Plan Authority
