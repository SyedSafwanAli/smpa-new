History

  • From 1968 to the present

How the Authority Took Shape

The Sindh Master Plan Authority is not a newly formed body. Its line runs back to a department of the Karachi Development Authority in 1968, and over half a century of changing names, changing control and a widening mandate it emerged in 2020 as a separate provincial authority.

  1. 1968

    Master Plan Department was established in 1968 under Karachi Development Authority (KDA) with the technical assistance of United Nations, during the preparation of Karachi Development Plan (1974-85).

  2. Late 1970s

    It was renamed as Master Plan & Environmental Control Department.

  3. 2000

    The Governing Body of KDA, meeting on 18-04-2000 under the Chairmanship of the Chief Secretary Sindh, resolved that a Consultant (Urban Planner) would be appointed for the capacity building of Master Plan Department, and that a board for its control would be established with representation of all the development agencies of Karachi.

  4. 2001

    Sindh Local Government Ordinance-2001 was promulgated and KDA was dissolved. Three of its departments — Master Plan & Environment Control, Design Bureau, and Planning & Urban Design — were merged into one and renamed Master Plan Group of Offices (MPGO), placed under City District Government Karachi.

  5. 2011

    The MPGO was placed under the control of Karachi Metropolitan Corporation (KMC) and renamed Master Plan Department (MPD).

  6. 2013

    The Master Plan Department was placed under the Administrative, Financial and Technical control of the Director General, Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA).

    No. SO(L&C)HTP/SBCA/12-14/2013 · 06-11-2013
  7. 2020

    Pursuing the orders of the Honourable Supreme Court of Pakistan dated 22-01-2019 in C.P 815-K/2016 and the decision of the Sindh Cabinet of 22 January 2020, a separate Sindh Master Plan Authority was established under the Local Government and Housing & Town Planning Department, Government of Sindh.

    No. SO(G)/HTP/SBCA/7-63/2018 · 18-02-2020
  8. 2020

    The Department of Town Planning, Government of Sindh — carrying the mandate of master planning for all districts of Sindh except Karachi division — was merged into the Sindh Master Plan Authority.

    No. SO(G)/HTP/SBCA/7-63/2018 · 14-09-2020
  9. 2022

    The Department of Planning and Urban Design (DPUD) was transferred from the Sindh Master Plan Authority and placed under the Administrative control of the Karachi Development Authority.

    No. SO(G)/HTP/KDA/8-23/2022 · 18-10-2022
