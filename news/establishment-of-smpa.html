Establishment of SMPA in Pursuance of the Supreme Court of Pakistan’s Order

  • 15 April 2026
  • Sindh Master Plan Authority

In pursuance of the Supreme Court of Pakistan’s Order dated 22-01-2019 and the subsequent Cabinet decision, the Master Plan Department was separated from SBCA and established as a separate Sindh Master Plan Authority vide letter dated 18-02-2020.

In pursuance of the Order of the Honourable Supreme Court of Pakistan dated 22-01-2019 in C.P 815-K/2016, and the decision of the Sindh Cabinet in its meeting held on 22 January 2020, a separate “Sindh Master Plan Authority” was established under the Local Government and Housing & Town Planning Department, Government of Sindh, vide Notification No. SO(G)/HTP/SBCA/7-63/2018 dated 18-02-2020.

Until then the Master Plan Department had been operating under the Administrative, Financial and Technical control of the Director General, Sindh Building Control Authority, under Notification No. SO(L&C)HTP/SBCA/12-14/2013 dated 06-11-2013.

The Authority carries responsibility for macro planning and master planning, the issuance of special development permits and changes of land-use — work that was previously the purview of the Master Plan & Environmental Control Department, the Master Plan Group of Offices and the Master Plan Department.
