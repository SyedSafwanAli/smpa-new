Grand Ceremony at Sindh Master Plan Authority on Independence Day, Cake Cutting Ceremony Held

  • 15 August 2026
  • Sindh Master Plan Authority

A ceremony was held at the Sindh Master Plan Authority to mark Independence Day, where a cake was cut in honour of 14 August and special prayers were offered for the security, progress and prosperity of Pakistan.

Karachi: A grand ceremony was held at the Sindh Master Plan Authority on the occasion of Independence Day celebrations, where a cake was cut in honour of Pakistan’s Independence Day, and special prayers were offered for the security, progress and prosperity of Pakistan.

The ceremony was attended by Senior Director Sindh Master Plan Authority Shakeel Siddiqui, Director General Karachi Development Authority Asif Jan Siddiqui, Member Finance KDA Naveed Anwar Siddiqui, Secretary KDA Muhammad Arshad Khan, Labour Union Supervisor Muhammad Ashraf, Director Admin and Finance Sindh Master Plan Authority Aijaz Sheikh, Director Coordination and Legal Orangzaib Qaim Khani, Asif Ahmad, Deputy Director Admin Ayaz Latif, Muddathir, Rashid Qaim Khani, Muslihuddin, Ali Tanveer and other staff members.

On this occasion, participants expressed national sentiments and patriotism, reaffirming their commitment to fulfil their responsibilities with greater dedication, honesty and sincerity towards nation-building, institutional improvement and public service.

August 14 reminds us of the great sacrifices and struggle of our forefathers for Pakistan’s creation. Independence is a great blessing, and its value and protection is our shared responsibility.

Shakeel Siddiqui, Senior Director, Sindh Master Plan Authority

Shakeel Siddiqui stated that Independence Day provides an opportunity to renew our role in the progress, prosperity, stability and national unity of the homeland. Following the principles of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah — Unity, Faith and Discipline — we should play our part in making Pakistan a stronger and more developed nation by incorporating these principles into our practical lives.
