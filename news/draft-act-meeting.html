Meeting on the Draft Act of Sindh Master Plan Authority (SMPA)

  • 15 April 2026
  • Sindh Master Plan Authority

Under the Chairmanship of Syed Nasir Hussain Shah, Minister for Local Government, Housing and Town Planning, Sindh, a high-level meeting was convened to review the progress of the drafted Sindh Master Plan Authority Act, aimed at establishing a robust, independent and modernized planning framework for the province.

The meeting was attended by Dr. Waseem Shamshad (Secretary Local Government), Director General (SBCA), DG (KDA), DG (LDA), DG (MDA), DG (SDA), DG (HDA), Shakeel Sidiqui, Senior Director Sindh Master Plan Authority (SMPA), and senior town planning experts including Dr. Noman, Pro-Vice Chancellor NED University of Engineering & Technology.

The Honourable Minister emphasized the role of master planning for the development of the urban and rural areas of Sindh. M. Orangzeb Qaimkhani, Director (Coord. & Legal) Sindh Master Plan Authority, gave a detailed briefing to the attendees on the legal and technical aspects and the current status of the legislation. The needs of the population and the cities were discussed at length.

Key highlights of the meeting

  1. Establishment of an independent authority The Minister emphasized that the primary objective of the draft act is to make the Sindh Master Plan Authority a fully autonomous body, to ensure impartial and scientific urban planning.
  2. Province-wide scope The proposed Act will empower the SMPA to plan at regional, district and city levels across the entire province, addressing the growing needs of both urban and rural areas.
  3. Fast-tracking the legislation Noting that the draft has been under process for several years, the Minister directed that all legal vetting be completed immediately to submit the final draft to the Sindh Cabinet for approval, aiming for tabling in the Provincial Assembly without further delay.
  4. Modernization and restructuring The new authority will incorporate specialized technical departments, including Urban Design & Renewal and the Design Bureau, to transform cities into smart, resilient and sustainable settlements.
  5. Integration of plans The meeting finalized that the authority will ensure all land-owning agencies, including Cantonment Boards, integrate their developmental activities with the overall master plan to prevent haphazard construction.

The establishment of the Sindh Master Plan Authority is a critical requirement of the Supreme Court and essential for the future of our province. We are transforming the planning regime from reactive to proactive, ensuring that Sindh’s development is sustainable, aesthetic and inclusive. The days of haphazard expansion are over; this Authority will provide a comprehensive roadmap for the future.

Syed Nasir Hussain Shah, Minister for Local Government, Housing and Town Planning, Sindh

The meeting concluded with a commitment to finalizing the legislation within the current, proactive reform agenda of the Local Government Department.
